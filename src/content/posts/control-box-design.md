## Overview

This project brings electrical documentation and mechanical packaging together in a control-box design and fabrication workflow.

## Implementation

- Draw circuit signal diagrams and power diagrams in KiCad.
- Model the internal control-box layout in SolidWorks.
- Prepare power and circuit layout drawings and 2D engineering drawings.
- Coordinate with a manufacturer and test the electrical circuits.
