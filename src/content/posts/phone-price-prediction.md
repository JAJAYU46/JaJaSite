## Overview

This project uses a trained machine learning model to estimate a phone's price from its key specifications. My documented focus was the relationship between brand and phone price.

## Implementation

The report compares models trained with and without the brand feature, using storage, RAM, screen size, and battery capacity as other inputs. A user interface accepts specifications and compares predicted prices across brands while holding the specifications constant.

The report discusses the limitations of using a small set of features. No individual example is presented here as a general prediction-accuracy benchmark.

## My Contributions

I analyzed the effect of brand on phone price. This work is identified as “The Effect of Brand on Phone Price” on pages 9–13 of the linked source document. Those pages were reviewed during this import; they document the brand-comparison workflow and interface. The broader multi-function project was team work.
